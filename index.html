About

I am a PhD student at IIT Ropar, where I started in January 2022. My research is on engagement- and affect-aware machine learning: recognising how engaged and attentive someone is from video, voice and conversation, and using those signals inside interactive systems.

I work with Dr. Jainendra Shukla at the Human-Machine Interaction Lab, IIIT-Delhi, and collaborate with Dr. Abhinav Dhall (Monash University). From January to June 2027 I will be a visiting researcher at JAIST, Japan, hosted by Prof. Shogo Okada.

Research

  1. Predicting engagement

    Interpretable multimodal models that estimate engagement from faces, gaze, voice and affect, and show which signals drove the prediction.

  2. Engagement is not attention

    Using the DREAMS dataset and video-language models to ask how engagement and attention relate, and where they come apart.

  3. Engagement in conversation

    Moving from single people to two-person interaction: predicting engagement as it rises and falls during a real conversation.

  4. Agents that act on it

    A conversational tutoring agent that reads engagement and affect while a learner works, and decides when and how to help.
